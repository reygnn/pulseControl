## Left as they are

- The cursor's scale comes from the window, not the cursor surface. wlroots 0.19
  sends the cursor surface its own scale, but mwc keeps a window on one output, so
  the two agree; elsewhere the window has the highest scale it touches, so the cursor
  is only ever too sharp. Following the cursor surface would reload in a loop with
  the empty buffer it gets between themes.
- The drag test keeps `sleep 0.2`. Without the refresh on release it failed three of
  three runs with the sleep and three of three without; it cannot fail falsely.
